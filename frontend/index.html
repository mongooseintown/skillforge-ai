Frequently Asked Questions

How does SkillForge AI generate personalized roadmaps? ✕
We combine Google AI Studio (Gemini 1.5 Flash/Pro) with RAG optimization, and fall back to locally running DeepSeek-R1 (1.5B / 7B via Ollama & Node.js).
Are certificates verifiable by employers? +
Yes! Every earned badge includes a unique cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature that employers can verify on a zero-trust public link without log in.
How are learning roadmaps generated? +
Roadmaps are dynamically constructed by indexing real-world job postings, official technical docs, and your personal diagnostic assessment scores.
Can I use local LLMs for AI tutoring? +
Absolutely. SkillForge includes full Node.js Express integration for local DeepSeek-R1 models running via Ollama on your computer.
Is there a free tier for students? +
Yes, our Starter Forge plan is free forever and includes 3 active AI career roadmaps, basic diagnostic audits, and standard verification badges.
